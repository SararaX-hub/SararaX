Instagram Settings

Set Up for High Performance

These adjustments take 5 minutes and can dramatically increase your reach. Do them once.

1

Switch to a Creator Account

Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account → Creator. Unlocks analytics, contact buttons, and category labels.

2

Turn On High-Quality Uploads

Settings → Account → Data Usage → turn on "Upload at Highest Quality." Your videos will look sharper in the feed immediately.

3

Add a Keyword to Your Name Field

Edit Profile → Name: Add your niche keyword (e.g. "Amara | Lifestyle & Wellness"). Instagram uses this field for search — very powerful.

4

Write a Bio with a Clear Call to Action

Say WHO you help, HOW you help them, and WHAT to do next (e.g. "↓ Free guide below"). Keep it under 150 characters.

5

Post at Your Peak Times

Go to Insights → Followers → scroll to "Most Active Times." Post within those hours for maximum organic reach.

6

Enable Cross-Sharing to Facebook

Settings → Account → Sharing to Other Apps → connect Facebook. Doubles your potential reach with zero extra effort.

7

Pin Your 3 Best Reels

Hold down on your top-performing Reels and tap "Pin to Profile." New visitors see your best work first — more follows guaranteed.

How to Shoot

Look Great on Camera — Every Time

Simple, practical techniques anyone can apply with just a phone. No fancy gear needed to start making great content.

📐 Camera Angles That Work ▼
  • Eye-Level: Camera at eye height — feels like a real conversation. Best for talking-head content.
  • Slightly Above Eye Level: Camera just above your face, angled slightly down. Most flattering for face and body — used by almost every creator.
  • Low Angle (Power Shot): Camera below you shooting upward. Makes you look confident and authoritative. Great for bold statements.
  • Over-the-Shoulder: Shows what you're working on or your surroundings. Adds context, makes viewers feel like they're with you.
  • Wide Shot: Shows your full body or environment. Great for outfits, room tours, cooking, or activity content.
  • Close-Up: Tight on your face or hands. Adds emotion and detail. Perfect for tutorials and reactions.
💡 Lighting Tips ▼
  • Natural light is free and the best: Face a window — don't have your back to it. Soft daylight is flattering and professional-looking.
  • Golden Hour: 1 hour after sunrise or before sunset gives warm, beautiful light outdoors. Perfect for lifestyle and fashion content.
  • Avoid harsh overhead lighting: Creates unflattering shadows under your eyes and nose.
  • Ring light tip: Position it at eye level, slightly to the side for depth. Dead centre can look flat.
  • Background lighting: Add a lamp behind you for depth — separates you from the background and looks more professional.
🎬 Framing & Composition ▼
  • Rule of Thirds: Don't always centre yourself. Position your face in the upper third of the frame — looks more dynamic.
  • Headroom: Leave a small gap between your head and the top of the frame. Too much or too little looks off.
  • Clean Background: Remove clutter. A plain wall, a shelf, or a neat space all work. Your audience should focus on YOU.
  • Create Depth: Stand a few feet in front of your background. Creates a blurred background (bokeh) that looks cinematic.
  • Always Film Vertical: Shoot in 9:16 portrait mode. Fill the whole frame — no black bars on Reels or TikTok.
🎙 Audio & Sound ▼
  • Bad audio kills good video: People will watch blurry video, but they will immediately leave if the sound is poor.
  • Quiet room first: Close windows, turn off fans, choose a room with soft furnishings — they absorb echo naturally.
  • Earphone mic trick: Plug in your basic phone earphones and speak clearly. The small inline mic is a massive free upgrade over your phone's built-in mic.
  • Music volume: Background music should sit well under your voice — around 20–30% volume max.
🎭 On-Camera Confidence ▼
  • Look at the lens, not the screen: The camera lens is the eye of your viewer. Looking there creates real connection.
  • Speak 20% more energetically than normal: Energy compresses on video. What feels big on camera often looks just right to viewers.
  • Slow down: Most new creators talk too fast. Pause between ideas — it builds tension and keeps viewers engaged.
  • Use your hands naturally: Hand gestures make you look confident and keep energy flowing in the frame.
  • Smile before you hit record: Relaxes your face and sets a warm, welcoming tone from the very first frame.
  • Do multiple takes: Your 3rd or 4th take is almost always better than your first. That's normal — not failure.

Advanced Hook Formulas

Word-for-word scripts used by creators with 100K+ followers.

FORMULA 01 — The Contradiction Hook
Say the opposite of what they expect

"Everyone tells you to post every day. I stopped posting for 3 weeks and my account grew faster than ever. Here is what happened."

💡 Why it works: Contradicts a belief they already hold. Forces them to stay and find out why.
FORMULA 02 — The Specific Number Hook
Use a precise number — never round

"I got 47,382 views from one Reel using this exact 3-second technique. I will show you the whole thing."

💡 Why it works: Specific numbers feel real and credible. "50K views" sounds made up. "47,382" sounds like a real result.
FORMULA 03 — The Confession Hook
Admit something uncomfortable first

"I am going to be honest — my first 50 videos were embarrassing. But video 51 changed everything and I need to show you what I changed."

💡 Why it works: Vulnerability creates trust instantly. People lean in when creators are honest about failure.
FORMULA 04 — The Time Pressure Hook
Create urgency around information

"This Instagram feature is working RIGHT NOW but most creators do not know about it yet. Once everyone finds out it will stop working — so watch this now."

💡 Why it works: FOMO. People fear missing something that is working right now.
FORMULA 05 — The Relatable Failure Hook
Start with the feeling they already have

"You spend 3 hours making a Reel. You post it. 47 views. All from your mum. I know exactly how that feels — and I know exactly what you are doing wrong."

💡 Why it works: Mirrors their exact experience. They feel seen and immediately trust you understand their problem.
FORMULA 06 — The Permission Hook
Give them permission to do the thing they are afraid of

"You do not need to be confident. You do not need expensive equipment. You do not even need a niche yet. You just need to start — and I will show you how."

💡 Why it works: Removes every excuse and objection before they can even think it.
FORMULA 07 — The Insider Secret Hook
Position information as exclusive knowledge

"What I am about to share with you is something most creators figure out only after 2 or 3 years. I am going to give it to you right now in the next 60 seconds."

💡 Why it works: Makes viewers feel they are getting privileged access to information others do not have.
FORMULA 08 — The Before/After Hook
Show the transformation upfront

"Six months ago I had 200 followers and zero brand deals. Today I have 18,000 followers and 4 paid partnerships. The only thing I changed was this one thing."

💡 Why it works: People buy transformations not information. Show the destination first and they will follow you on the journey.

As a general guide: Under 5K followers — TSh 50,000 to 150,000 per post. 5K to 20K followers — TSh 150,000 to 500,000 per post. 20K to 50K followers — TSh 500,000 to 1,500,000 per post. Always charge more for Reels than stories. Always charge more for exclusive usage rights. Never work for free — even gifted products should have a cash fee attached.

💡 These are starting points. Your engagement rate matters more than your follower count. 5K highly engaged followers can charge more than 50K passive followers.

Creator Media Kit Guide

Build a media kit that makes brands take you seriously.

WHAT IS A MEDIA KIT
Your professional creator CV

A media kit is a 1-2 page document that tells brands everything they need to know about you as a creator — your audience, your numbers, your content style, and your rates. Think of it as your CV for brand partnerships. Without one, brands will not take you seriously. With one, you look like a professional even with small numbers.

SECTION 1 — YOUR INTRODUCTION
Who you are in 3 sentences

Write: Your name and handle. Your niche in one sentence. Your audience in one sentence. Example: "I am Amara, a lifestyle and wellness creator based in Dar es Salaam. I create content about simple, affordable healthy living for everyday Tanzanians. My audience is primarily women aged 18-35 who want to live better without spending more."

💡 Be specific about your location and audience. Local relevance is your competitive advantage over international creators.
SECTION 2 — YOUR NUMBERS
Include these exact stats

Total followers across platforms. Monthly reach or impressions. Average engagement rate (likes + comments divided by followers x 100). Average views per Reel. Your best performing post stats. Audience demographics if you have them (age, gender, location). Update these every month — never use outdated numbers.

💡 Engagement rate is more important than follower count. A 10% engagement rate with 2,000 followers is more valuable to brands than 1% with 20,000.
SECTION 3 — YOUR SERVICES AND RATES
What you offer and what you charge

List clearly: Instagram Reel (sponsored) — your price. Instagram Story (3 slides) — your price. Instagram static post — your price. UGC content (raw file, no posting) — your price. Content package (Reel + story + static) — bundled price. Long-term ambassador deal — negotiate separately. Always include a note that rates are negotiable and you are open to gifted collaborations with a cash fee.

SECTION 4 — PAST WORK AND TESTIMONIALS
Social proof closes deals

If you have worked with brands before — even gifted collabs — include screenshots or logos. If you have positive comments from followers about products you have recommended, screenshot those too. If this is your first collaboration, write: "Open to first collaborations with brands whose values align with my content." Never lie about past work — the internet is small.

💡 Build your media kit on Canva for free. Search "media kit" in Canva templates and customise one to match your brand colours.

HOW TO SPOT A SCAM DEAL
Protect yourself as a creator

Real brands will: Pay you before or at delivery. Have a real website and social presence. Give you a clear brief. Never ask you to pay anything upfront. Scam deals will: Ask you to buy the product first and "get reimbursed." Have no contract or agreement. Offer unrealistically high payment. Pressure you to decide immediately. If something feels wrong, trust your gut and walk away.

💡 Always get agreement in writing — even a WhatsApp message confirming the deal terms is better than nothing.

Caption Formulas That Convert

Captions that make people comment, save, and follow.

FORMULA 01 — The Hook-Story-CTA
The most reliable caption structure

HOOK: [Grab attention in 1-2 lines — make them stop]

STORY: [2-3 sentences of context, personal detail, or the problem]

VALUE: [The main point, lesson, or takeaway]

CTA: [One clear action — save this, comment below, follow for more]

Example:
I almost quit content creation 3 months ago.

I was posting every day, getting 200 views, and watching creators I started with hit 100K followers. I felt invisible.

Then I changed one thing — I stopped making content FOR the algorithm and started making it FOR my audience. Everything changed.

Save this if you ever feel like giving up. It gets better — but only if you stay consistent 🙏

💡 Your first line is everything. If the first line does not stop them, they will never read the rest.
FORMULA 02 — The List Caption
Easy to read, highly saveable

Start with a bold statement. Then list 3-5 points numbered clearly. End with a save CTA. Example: "5 things I stopped doing that doubled my engagement: 1. Posting without a hook. 2. Using generic hashtags. 3. Ignoring comments in the first hour. 4. Posting at random times. 5. Making content for everyone instead of someone specific. Save this — you will need it."

💡 List captions get saved 3x more than paragraph captions. People love to reference lists later.
FORMULA 03 — The Question Caption
Drives comments and conversation

State your opinion or experience. Then ask your audience a direct question at the end. Make the question easy and low-effort to answer. Example: "I used to think I needed 10K followers before I could make money from content. I got my first brand deal at 800 followers. What is the biggest myth you believed about content creation? Drop it below 👇"

💡 Comments boost your reach more than likes. Questions are the easiest way to get comments.
HASHTAG STRATEGY
How many and which ones

Use 5 to 10 hashtags maximum — not 30. Mix three sizes: 2 large hashtags (1M+ posts) — for reach. 3 medium hashtags (100K to 500K posts) — for discoverability. 3 small or niche hashtags (under 50K posts) — for ranking. Always include 1 to 2 location hashtags if you are targeting a local audience. Example for a Tanzanian lifestyle creator: #lifestyletz #tanzaniancreator #darsalaam #africanlifestyle #contentcreatortanzania.

💡 Put hashtags in the caption, not the comments. Instagram confirmed captions get better reach than comment hashtags.
