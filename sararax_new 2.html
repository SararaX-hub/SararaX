How to Shoot Like a Pro

Great content is not about expensive cameras. It is about knowing the rules — angles, lighting, movement, and story.

📐 Framing & Angles ▼
Rule of thirds: Put yourself slightly off-center in the frame — not dead centre. Your eyes should land in the top third of the screen.

Always Film Vertical: Shoot in 9:16 portrait mode. Fill the whole frame — no black bars on Reels.

Eye level or above: Camera at eye level or slightly above. Never shoot from below — it is unflattering and unprofessional.

Leave headroom: Your head should not touch the top of the frame. Leave a small gap.

Close-up power: Your face filling the frame creates intimacy and connection. Use it for emotional or storytelling moments.
💡 Lighting Setup ▼
Golden rule: Light source in FRONT of you, never behind. If the window is behind you, you become a silhouette.

Best times for natural light: 7–10am and 4–7pm. Avoid harsh midday sun — it creates unflattering shadows.

Ring light placement: Position it directly in front of your face at eye level for the cleanest look.

Avoid mixed lighting: Do not mix warm bulbs and cool daylight. All light in your shot should be the same temperature.

Diffusion hack: Tape white paper or a white sheet over your ring light to soften harsh shadows.
📱 Camera Settings ▼
Resolution: Shoot at the highest resolution your phone allows — 4K if possible, minimum 1080p.

Frame rate: 30fps for normal content. 60fps if you want smooth slow-motion clips.

Stabilisation: Enable OIS or EIS stabilisation in settings. Or use a gimbal for walking shots.

Rear camera: The rear camera is almost always higher quality than the selfie camera. Use it when you can — set up your tripod and use a timer or bluetooth remote.

Grid lines: Turn on grid lines in camera settings to help with rule-of-thirds framing.
🎵 Audio Quality ▼
Audio matters more than video. People will watch blurry video with clear audio. They will not watch crystal clear video with bad audio.

Film in a quiet space: Close windows, turn off fans, move away from roads. Ambient noise ruins audio.

Soft surfaces absorb echo: Film in a room with carpets, curtains, or cushions. Avoid empty rooms with hard walls.

Distance from mic: With a clip-on mic, position it 15–20cm from your mouth. With phone audio, stay within 50cm of the phone.

Test before filming: Always record a 5-second test clip and listen back before filming your full video.
🎭 On-Camera Presence ▼
Look at the lens, not the screen. Looking at the screen feels like you are looking away. Train yourself to look directly at the camera lens.

Speak slightly faster than normal. On camera, you naturally come across slower. Speed up your natural pace by 20%.

Energy up: Stand up when filming if you can. Standing naturally increases your energy and projection.

Mistakes are fine: Start filming before you are ready. The first take is always your worst — film 5–10 takes and the best one will emerge.

Edit your pauses: Remove all pauses, filler words, and dead air in editing. A tight, pacey video keeps people watching.

Advanced Hook Formulas

Word-for-word scripts used by creators with 100K+ followers.

FORMULA 01 — The Contradiction Hook
Say the opposite of what they expect

"Everyone tells you to post every day. I stopped posting for 3 weeks and my account grew faster than ever. Here is what happened."

💡 Why it works: Contradicts a belief they already hold. Forces them to stay and find out why.
FORMULA 02 — The Specific Number Hook
Use a precise number — never round

"I got 47,382 views from one Reel using this exact 3-second technique. I will show you the whole thing."

💡 Why it works: Specific numbers feel real and credible. "50K views" sounds made up. "47,382" sounds like a real result.
FORMULA 03 — The Confession Hook
Admit something uncomfortable first

"I am going to be honest — my first 50 videos were embarrassing. But video 51 changed everything and I need to show you what I changed."

💡 Why it works: Vulnerability creates trust instantly. People lean in when creators are honest about failure.
FORMULA 04 — The Time Pressure Hook
Create urgency around information

"This Instagram feature is working RIGHT NOW but most creators do not know about it yet. Once everyone finds out it will stop working — so watch this now."

💡 Why it works: FOMO. People fear missing something that is working right now.
FORMULA 05 — The Relatable Failure Hook
Start with the feeling they already have

"You spend 3 hours making a Reel. You post it. 47 views. All from your mum. I know exactly how that feels — and I know exactly what you are doing wrong."

💡 Why it works: Mirrors their exact experience. They feel seen and immediately trust you understand their problem.
FORMULA 06 — The Permission Hook
Give them permission to do the thing they are afraid of

"You do not need to be confident. You do not need expensive equipment. You do not even need a niche yet. You just need to start — and I will show you how."

💡 Why it works: Removes every excuse and objection before they can even think it.
FORMULA 07 — The Insider Secret Hook
Position information as exclusive knowledge

"What I am about to share with you is something most creators figure out only after 2 or 3 years. I am going to give it to you right now in the next 60 seconds."

💡 Why it works: Makes viewers feel they are getting privileged access to information others do not have.
FORMULA 08 — The Before/After Hook
Show the transformation upfront

"Six months ago I had 200 followers and zero brand deals. Today I have 18,000 followers and 4 paid partnerships. The only thing I changed was this one thing."

💡 Why it works: People buy transformations not information. Show the destination first and they will follow you on the journey.

Creator Media Kit Guide

Build a media kit that makes brands take you seriously.

WHAT IS A MEDIA KIT
Your professional creator CV

A media kit is a 1-2 page document that tells brands everything they need to know about you as a creator — your audience, your numbers, your content style, and your rates. Without one, brands will not take you seriously. With one, you look like a professional even with small numbers.

SECTION 1 — YOUR INTRODUCTION
Who you are in 3 sentences

Write: Your name and handle. Your niche in one sentence. Your audience in one sentence. Example: "I am Amara, a lifestyle and wellness creator based in Dar es Salaam. I create content about simple, affordable healthy living for everyday Tanzanians. My audience is primarily women aged 18-35."

💡 Be specific about your location and audience. Local relevance is your competitive advantage over international creators.
SECTION 2 — YOUR NUMBERS
Include these exact stats

Total followers across platforms. Monthly reach or impressions. Average engagement rate (likes + comments divided by followers x 100). Average views per Reel. Your best performing post stats. Audience demographics if you have them. Update these every month — never use outdated numbers.

💡 Engagement rate is more important than follower count. A 10% engagement rate with 2,000 followers is more valuable than 1% with 20,000.
SECTION 3 — YOUR SERVICES AND RATES
What you offer and what you charge

List clearly: Instagram Reel (sponsored) — your price. Instagram Story (3 slides) — your price. Instagram static post — your price. UGC content (raw file, no posting) — your price. Content package — bundled price. Long-term ambassador deal — negotiate separately.

SECTION 4 — PAST WORK AND TESTIMONIALS
Social proof closes deals

If you have worked with brands before — even gifted collabs — include screenshots or logos. If you have positive comments from followers about products you have recommended, screenshot those too. If this is your first collaboration, write: "Open to first collaborations with brands whose values align with my content."

Caption Formulas That Convert

Captions that make people comment, save, and follow.

FORMULA 01 — The Hook-Story-CTA
The most reliable caption structure

HOOK: [Grab attention in 1-2 lines — make them stop]

STORY: [2-3 sentences of context, personal detail, or the problem]

VALUE: [The main point, lesson, or takeaway]

CTA: [One clear action — save this, comment below, follow for more]

💡 Your first line is everything. If the first line does not stop them, they will never read the rest.
FORMULA 02 — The List Caption
Easy to read, highly saveable

Start with a bold statement. Then list 3-5 points numbered clearly. End with a save CTA. Example: "5 things I stopped doing that doubled my engagement: 1. Posting without a hook. 2. Using generic hashtags. 3. Ignoring comments in the first hour. 4. Posting at random times. 5. Making content for everyone instead of someone specific. Save this — you will need it."

💡 List captions get saved 3x more than paragraph captions.
FORMULA 03 — The Question Caption
Drives comments and conversation

State your opinion or experience. Then ask your audience a direct question at the end. Make the question easy and low-effort to answer. Example: "I got my first brand deal at 800 followers. What is the biggest myth you believed about content creation? Drop it below 👇"

💡 Comments boost your reach more than likes. Questions are the easiest way to get comments.
HASHTAG STRATEGY
How many and which ones

Use 5 to 10 hashtags maximum — not 30. Mix three sizes: 2 large hashtags (1M+ posts) — for reach. 3 medium hashtags (100K to 500K posts) — for discoverability. 3 small or niche hashtags (under 50K posts) — for ranking. Always include 1 to 2 location hashtags. Example: #lifestyletz #tanzaniancreator #darsalaam #africanlifestyle #contentcreatortanzania.

💡 Put hashtags in the caption, not the comments. Instagram confirmed captions get better reach.
